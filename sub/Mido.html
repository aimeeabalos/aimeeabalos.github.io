Mido Cloud Series

"I see you, I cherish you and I carry you with me"

A 3D modeled IP character design inspired by me, my family, and my friends. This 3D collectible character series transform the people I know into playful, stylized figures. Each design is a blend of their unique personality and interests.

The design process is deeply personal (from the sketches, design, choose of elements until I 3D modeled them). I design based on how they have influenced who I am.

Most crucial part, Color palettes(very important to mention) directly inspired by gradients soft hues of sunrise to the deep tones of sundown.

I know you can understand: You are someone's sun, someone's sunrise, someone's sundown, someone's whole sky.
